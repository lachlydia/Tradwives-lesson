SEXI training AFB II

A convincing argument has four parts. Train each part before you build your own.

S

Statement – your claim in one clear sentence.

E

Explanation – why is it true?

X

eXample – proof: a line from the text, a fact, a case.

I

Illustration – a picture the audience can see: “Imagine …”, “It's like …”
